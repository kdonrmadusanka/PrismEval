# Claims and Compliance

Every factual claim must be verifiable. Do not state statistics, rankings, results or outcomes unless the source is provided or clearly known.

Avoid absolute or guaranteed language such as "always", "never", "100%", "guaranteed", "absolutely" or "proven" unless there is evidence to support it.

Do not make promises about results, grades, income, health outcomes or job placement. Use measured wording such as "can help", "is designed to" or "many participants report".

Comparisons with competitors or other programs must be fair, specific and supportable. Do not disparage others.

Do not use false urgency or pressure, such as fake deadlines or "only today", to push a decision.

Content must not misrepresent who is speaking, or imply an endorsement that has not been given.
